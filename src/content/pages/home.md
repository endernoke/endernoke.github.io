I like building things that empower people. Some started as accessibility tools that I needed myself. Others include cool open-source terminal apps and tools. A few of them are on the [projects](/projects) page.

I study Financial Technology at the University of Hong Kong, and previously interned at the City University of Hong Kong, where I did research on keeping AI agents safe when they operate graphical interfaces.

Away from the editor, I [travel](/travels), read, and try out random cool things. I enjoy learning by treading unfamiliar territory, like organizing a high school hackathon from scratch while competing in olympiads well beyond my usual interests.

I write here about technology, creation, and what building things has taught me.
