Away from the editor, you can find me travelling, reading, and trying out random cool things. I enjoy learning by tredding unfamiliar territory, like organizing a high school hackathon from scratch while competing in olympiads well beyond my usual interests. I keep some of my [ideas and work in progress](/misc) here too.
