Walking into an unfamiliar building is a small act of faith for a lot of blind and low-vision people. Where is the door, the stairs, the counter? TouchSpace tries to answer those questions before the visit.

A phone scans the room with RoomPlan. From the scan we build a tactile map that can be 3D printed or shown on a refreshable pin display, with spatial audio and spoken questions for the parts that are easier to hear than to feel.

We built it over a weekend at Cal Hacks 13.0. The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/holokcheung/touchspace).
